Bouncing hedgehog is a game about a hedgehog who is trying to find a way home.(SPOILER:he never will)
All art in this game is made by hand.
this game was also inspired by flappy bird and the running dino when we didn't have internet access.
We also made trash (soda cans) elements to spread awareness about global problems cause a lot of people leave their trash outside and not in the bin.
It's just an easy way to spend your free time.:)
